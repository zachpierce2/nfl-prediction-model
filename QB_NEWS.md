# Official QB announcement adapter

The collector now reads official-club NewsArticle schema and accepts only an explicit starter decision with a full quarterback name, exact regular-season week and opponent in one statement (or an explicit own-team/week/season designation that maps to one scheduled game), in the headline or first three paragraphs. Current nflverse QB identities provide GSIS IDs and team/season checks. Speculation, questions, conditions, missing identities, unsupported schema, old games, other seasons and after-kickoff observations abstain. A team-wide starter designation without an explicit game is insufficient.

Every accepted source is fetched again while its game remains upcoming, even if it leaves RSS. Changed or missing decisions and unavailable sources revoke prior automatic confirmations; explicit negative decisions create unknown rows. Identical re-observations do not refresh old reports or override newer publications. Conflicting player identities at the latest publication become unknown. This is a narrow deterministic adapter, not a comprehensive news interpretation system; unfamiliar wording and unrelated injury reports may not be understood. Source changes between polls remain unobserved until the next run.

`python collect_qb_inputs.py` collects the feeds and article evidence, snapshots current player/schedule inputs, and writes a separate append-only automatic ledger in `data/auto_market/qb_announcements/`. A combined as-of CSV preserves the manual confirmation file and supplies `QB_CONFIRMATIONS_PATH` to the existing shadow forecast consumers. It never rewrites manual reports; a manually managed game/team keeps exclusive manual authority. Existing first-forecast/no-backfill behavior remains: a starter announcement after a game's first core forecast cannot create an old paired forecast.

The automatic-market workflow collects before prediction, persists the small ledger and coverage summary with its existing archive commit, and uploads raw article/feed/identity evidence as 90-day artifacts. A collection failure is surfaced and the forecast run falls back to manual confirmations only. The standalone QB news workflow is diagnostic and stores artifacts without committing its ledger. PRs #4 and #8 are merged on the default branch. Automatic collection is active every three hours, with daily grading; the activation collection and grading runs both passed.

Coverage includes all upcoming quoted games over eight days, showing missing confirmations; schedule coverage is reported separately. Accepted confirmation counts are not forecast counts or evidence of improved ATS accuracy. The historical model, baseline and existing predictions are unchanged. Grading now also reports the exact same-quote EPA/QB subset, without altering pick outcomes.

Validation: `python -m unittest -v test_qb_announcements test_qb_news test_qb_market test_qb_passing test_epa_shadow`. Tests cover identity/game matching, conditions and speculation, ambiguous IDs, retractions, missing-source revocation, publication/observation/kickoff boundaries, old-report ordering and existing paired forecasting/grading.

The v2 rule adds verified official-club phrasing naming a quarterback as the team starter heading into an explicit week and season. It requires a unique scheduled team/week game, preserving rejection of prior weeks and conflicting schedules. The observed Raiders Week 10 announcement is a negative current-game example, not a confirmation for Week 11. QB grading now also reports the exact same-quote EPA-versus-QB subset, its missing-baseline count, ATS accuracy and margin MAE.
